02 / YOUR ALGORITHM

JavaScript controller

INIT() + CONTROLLER()
Choose a controller and press Run.
Student API reference

init() runs once per experiment. controller() runs once every params.dt and returns absolute airway pressure in cmH₂O (or {pressure: …}). The simulator enforces PEEP ≤ pressure ≤ maxPressure. The "Driving pressure" and "Headroom" diagnostics use the applied pressure, not the unclipped request. To expose raw demand when you clamp internally for anti-windup, return {pressure, rawPressure: raw}. A plain number or {pressure: ...} still works, but cannot reveal a hidden pre-clamp demand.

Read-only state: volume (L), flow (L/s), pressure (cmH₂O), target.volume (L), target.flow (L/s), error (L), time (s), phase (inspiration/expiration), breath.

Read-only params: dt, compliance, resistance, peep, maxPressure, tidalVolume, breathsPerMin, inspirationFraction.

Writable memory: begins as {} for each experiment. Store integrators and histories here; do not change the simulated plant state.

THE PLANT

One compliance. One resistance.

Airway pressure is the manipulated input; lung volume above the selected PEEP baseline is the controlled output. The simplified model does not include PEEP-dependent recruitment or changes in lung compliance.

THE CHALLENGE

Not every reference is reachable.

Expiration is passive in this model. Pressure cannot fall below PEEP. A low ceiling or high airway resistance can prevent accurate volume tracking—even with a well-written controller.

EDUCATIONAL MODEL ONLY

Not a medical device.

No oxygen exchange, patient effort, clinical-grade alarms, valves, leaks, nonlinear physiology, or independent safety systems are modeled. The low-volume flag is an educational diagnostic only. Never use this lab to control a real ventilator or make treatment decisions.
